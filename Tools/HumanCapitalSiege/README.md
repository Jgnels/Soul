# Human Capital Siege V0

Use `play_siege.cmd` only with the accepted stage pinned by `playtest.json` and the current siege HANDOFF. The original Heartland launcher and stage remain separate.

- Choose **N** for an isolated start: Human army at Crossroads, Dwarf occupation of fortified Human Capital. Existing 45 Human bodies are split into infantry, archers and guards; no extra force is granted.
- Press **F5 at Crossroads before attacking** if you want a retry checkpoint. Select your army, then Human Capital, then **Attack**. The Siege V0 flag routes this fortified hostile capital to its actual gate, not the outer field battle.
- **P** pauses/unpauses; **C** switches hero/commander camera; **X** changes hero view; **WASD** moves the hero; **LMB** attacks; **RMB** guards in hero mode.
- Select troop cards or **All**. **H** holds, **G** charges, **V** follows the hero. Use **Move** and a clear ground waypoint. **R** returns formations to AI control.
- Strike the native portcullis with the hero or issue **Charge**. Its integrity must reach zero before passage opens. Enter the courtyard ring and hold it uncontested for 15 seconds, or defeat/rout the defending army. A breach alone is not victory.
- **F5/F9 are campaign save/load controls after return.** Mid-battle save/resume is not implemented; battle F-keys still belong to formation selection. **Continue** resumes the most recent F5 checkpoint in this separate session.
- Default review is capped at 30 FPS / 1280×800 with the existing **85°C hard guard**. This is not sustained-performance qualification. Keep the launcher console open. Save in campaign, then exit with **Alt+F4**.

Siege damage persists through the existing settlement domain. Damaged walls remain damaged on subsequent assaults; no free repair is introduced. Gate opening is a simple disappearance at zero integrity, not physics destruction. No ladders or siege engines are provided in V0.

A defeated, captured Aurora cannot launch another siege. Existing rescue/ransom is not implemented; restore the pre-assault checkpoint or choose a new isolated session to retry. Fortification tier 2 raises gate integrity from 1,000 to 1,300 in the rules, but a purchased wall-upgrade playthrough is not qualified.
