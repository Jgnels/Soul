# Campaign visual iterations

All observations below come from opened Unreal screenshots. Rejected runs remain in ignored Local logs; they are not counted as successful runtime acceptance.

1. Baseline: unmodified committed baseline binaries at 1280x720, `before-1280.png`. Obvious sphere places, debug links, dark void and large Canvas transcript.
2. Pass 1: `pass1-1280.png`. Continuous geometry established, but missing rendered material shaders caused gray/checkerboard fallback. Rejected. Generated only two source-owned campaign materials with real-RHI shader compilation and reversed camera side to clear the capital from HUD.
3. Pass 2: `pass2-1280.png`, `pass2-frontier-1280.png` and local shrine capture. Input/recruit/save/load qualification passed. Terrain washed out, road backfaces black, river discontinuous, terrain edge exposed. Corrected UE Create/Update mesh vertex-color conversion mismatch, winding, label orientation, extent and exposure.
4. Pass 3: `pass3-1280.png`, `pass3-frontier-1280.png`. Colors, roads and rocks corrected; 1280x720 input qualification passed. River still occluded by coarse terrain, exploration veil angular. Widened riverbed and bridge, blended environmental knowledge colors while retaining exact information gates, improved footer contrast and defeat guidance.
5. Pass 4 requested 1920x1080 but UE clamped its viewport to 888x500. PNG dimensions verified; rejected. Hostile mouse selection was blocked by HUD at that size. Repeated with `-ForceRes`. Pass 4b (`pass4-1920.png`) verified actual 1920x1080 and passed normal input qualification. Continuous river and softer exploration veil visible; settlement framing and silhouettes remained worth improving.
6. Pass 5: closer initial framing exposed a qualification cursor target under the real HUD; the click was correctly blocked. Rejected. Qualification now focuses the intended watch before clicking, preserving panel protection. Fixed directional fill priority and refined camera framing.
7. Pass 6: town HUD click, orbit/minimum/maximum zoom and Home assertions passed. Close/wide screenshot inspection exposed motion blur and the far terrain edge. Disabled campaign motion blur, separated capture from the next camera action and expanded continuous terrain to 14k half extents.
8. Pass 7: settled close/wide views now showed no edge. Oversized near labels, flat roofs and raised-road shadow strips remained. Added distance-scaled label size, pitched roofs, slight road width/color variation and disabled road shadow casting. Measured about 29.20 FPS under the intentional 30 FPS cap, including capture stalls.
9. Pass 8: middle-mouse drag and full input sequence passed. Close/town/shrine inspections confirmed improved labels and party helmets/shields, then revealed an inverted roof pitch. Corrected roof roll sign, added restrained ambient material contribution and moved garrison silhouettes outside fort walls. Company cosmetic children now hide when its army is empty.
10. Final visual runs at both actual 1280x720 and 1920x1080 passed. A final action-message guard was added to keep remembered distant selection from revealing current garrison status, then both resolutions were qualified again (`qualified-1280`, `qualified-1920`). All assertions passed, exit 0, normal shutdown. Final initial/frontier/shrine/prompt/close/wide images were inspected: no graph spheres/debug roads, readable river/roads/places and subordinate HUD. Initial fog preserves unexplored place information; later exploration visibly opens the world.
11. Final real stress victory and two-encounter defeat/recruit/retry were rendered and inspected. `dragon-battle-final-1280.png` shows actual Dragon Graveyard combat; `roundtrip-return-final-1280.png` shows six survivors and human ownership after conquest; `stronghold-final-1280.png` shows the revealed hostile destination; `recovery-return-final-1280.png` correctly shows no fighting figures and recruitment guidance after defeat.

The accepted style is deliberately simple, stylized miniature art. Remaining authored-art and production gaps are stated in HANDOFF.md. The evidence demonstrates a category change and working gameplay, not AAA parity. Full logs and telemetry remain in ignored Local/; compact final receipts are committed beside this file.
