# Source and scale decision — candidate NOT accepted for production

Recommend **3.5 × 3.5 km as the next working physical footprint**, with native Mountain05 as the strongest directly verified heightform source. Do **not** promote `/Game/SoulCampaignFoundation/L_Mountain05_3500_r1`: its water/role layout and surface presentation fail visual acceptance. This is an evidence-backed scale recommendation and useful source study, not final source approval or a finished world.

FreshCan remains unavailable after the bounded local/cache/owned-download inspection. No authenticated CLI session and no usable launcher download controls were available. Its publisher imagery is contextual only: no numerical comparison or local performance claim is possible. Mountain05 is therefore the strongest directly verified source, not proven superior to an uninspected FreshCan source.

The lower-water 3.5 km arrangement accommodates 36 canonical node proposals. Median nearest-region distance is 375 m; the Human capital has 409 m to its nearest region, compared with its measured 69.70 × 51.44 m core. The retained Human-to-Crossroads separation is approximately 156 m. Dwarf Hold remains 30.52 × 38.69 m, with 732 m to its nearest proposed region. A 200 m Human hinterland is 92% dry ground below 15 degrees **at the provisional lower datum**, with 0.54–13.66 m elevation across its 5th–95th percentiles. No flattening was applied.

Matched 320 m camera views confirm an important limit: a larger world does not itself make the capital smaller on screen at the same local zoom. It provides more land and approach distance between sites. Keep the existing miniature scale; do not use a zoomed-out comparison to claim an art improvement.

The same 4 km study adds 31% area. Median nearest-region distance becomes 448 m and the Human nearest-region distance 455 m. It does not cure the long terrain detours; the maximum remains about 3.8 times endpoint distance. Increasing area is not justified by these results. The 3.5 km layout is sufficient for a rendered scale test, not yet approved final placement.

A water-plane sensitivity study lowers the native datum by 5 m, equivalent to 2.14 m in the candidate. Water coverage falls from 18.08% to 13.66%; shallow channels become land. All 51 rendered road strips were fitted to this provisional configuration. Native collision verifies maximum centerline grade 20.52°, but some shoulders reach 26.11°. Roads are trimmed before miniatures and are not qualified gate approaches.

Restoring the original water datum submerges portions of **28 existing study paths**. A separate dense dry-route search at the original datum finds only **38/51** paths. The original-water coarse search had found 46/51, demonstrating why coarse success is insufficient. Lowering water is not accepted as a way to hide crossing-design work.

The proposed Viking harbor is 27 m from an **enclosed lake**, not an ocean-connected harbor. River Ford faces a broad sea-connected inlet and is not a river crossing. Nearby short crossing opportunities support further Southern Crossing study, but the nearest shortlisted narrow-water opportunities for River Ford and Broken Bridge are roughly 0.5–0.6 km away. The Heart River and Eastern Run are not physically authored. These are material composition defects.

Twenty additional analytical framings of 6.12 km source windows at 3.5 km physical scale find 39–45 coarse dry paths. None exceeds the full domain's 46/51 original-water coarse result. The crop with 45 paths also puts the Dwarf site only 3.1 m above water and the Viking site 68.3 m above water. No crop was imported, and a higher path count was not treated as source acceptance.

The bounded map uses 2041² samples, 8 × 8 Landscape components, 255 quads per component, approximately 1.72 m sample spacing. Every sample of the surviving 2041² full-domain export is preserved, with a 90° rotation and uniform XYZ compression. This is **not** a copy of all 8161² native vertices. The native donor has 1024 components; its two bounded editor inspections reached the unchanged 12,000 MiB private-commit guard. The candidate's saved content passed a fresh-process load and native collision checks. No World Partition conversion is needed for this bounded test; authored cities remain separate locations.

Physical size alone is not a GPU budget: 3.5 and 4 km can use the same vertex/component count. The larger footprint needs more dressing at equal density and wider views can expose more content. Capped editor observations are not a 40+ FPS performance result. No uncapped run was justified while the visual gates failed; the 85 C guard remains unchanged.

Native paint weights were not recovered. The candidate uses a Soul-owned copy of the donor's automatic slope branch and original conventional textures, with provisional instance adjustments. It is not an exact native surface reproduction. A second source-only attempt produced two incomplete captures while texture builds were running, then reached the memory guard; the modern `render_weightmaps` API was identified but no export completed. Do not present those incomplete images as definitive source material quality.

The rendered candidate preserves stronger continuous relief than rejected R10, but does **not** establish a better finished visual world than the retained Soul slice. Woodland is sparse/stippled, water is provisional, map edges are abrupt, and the six regional identities are incomplete. Only existing Human/Dwarf and representative minor/army scale objects are present. No campaign input, movement, RBSave or battle integration was added for this candidate.

Next: inspect the exact FreshCan source when accessible, then resolve an explicit 3.5 km coast/river/crossing and faction-role layout before more terrain authoring. Retain Mountain05's authored relief as the verified fallback. Preserve the current reference map and all study iterations. No promotion, push or merge.
