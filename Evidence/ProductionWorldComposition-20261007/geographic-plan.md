## Jeff's geographic siting guidance

Bridges use the shortest practical span between stable opposing banks; roads bend into that crossing. Do not derive bridge length/orientation from an arbitrary route intersection. Natural sheltered harbors and trade-route chokepoints are favorable city sites.
