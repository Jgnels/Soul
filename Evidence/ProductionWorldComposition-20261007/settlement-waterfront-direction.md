# Settlement siting and bridge direction — Jeff's current clarifications

- Bridges should use the shortest practical stable bank-to-bank span. Fit road approaches to that span rather than extending a bridge along an arbitrary route line.
- Natural harbors and chokepoints on trade routes are favorable city sites. Capitals often develop beside meaningful rivers. Siting should account for water, trade, safe buildable land and hinterland together.
- The actual Hivemind Human Capital is a lake/water-island composition. Keep its full authored environment intact. Its campaign miniature needs corresponding lakeside/waterfront geography; do not invent an unrelated inland capital.
- The approved Viking Capital is a harbor environment. Its campaign site needs a sheltered ocean-connected harbor.
- Several approved Human town demos also have waterfront settings. Preserve those identities when assigning campaign sites; do not force every town into an inland generic footprint.
